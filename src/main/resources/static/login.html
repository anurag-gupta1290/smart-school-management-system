About Smart School

Smart School Management System is a next-generation, cloud-based educational ERP platform that empowers schools, colleges, and universities to manage their entire ecosystem digitally. From student admissions to academic performance tracking, from fee management to parent-teacher communication — everything is unified in one intelligent, secure, and intuitive platform.

Key Capabilities
  • Student Information System — Complete digital profiles with academic history, documents, and progress tracking.
  • Attendance Management — Biometric, RFID, and manual attendance with real-time parent notifications.
  • Examination & Grading — Automated grade calculation, report cards, and performance analytics.
  • Fee & Finance — Online payments, invoicing, scholarships, and financial reporting.
  • Communication Hub — SMS, email, push notifications, and in-app messaging for all stakeholders.
  • Library & Inventory — Digital cataloging, issue/return tracking, and asset management.
  • Transport & Hostel — Route optimization, GPS tracking, and hostel room allocation.
  • Analytics & Reports — Interactive dashboards with actionable insights for administrators.

Simple Process

How It Works

Getting started with Smart School is quick and easy. Follow these simple steps to digitize your institution.

1

Institution Registration

School administration registers the institution on our platform. Configure academic years, classes, sections, subjects, and grading systems according to your requirements.

2

User Onboarding

Import or create accounts for teachers, students, and parents. Bulk upload via CSV/Excel. Each user receives secure login credentials via email/SMS.

3

Data Configuration

Set up timetables, exam schedules, fee structures, transport routes, and other institutional data. Our team assists with migration from existing systems.

4

Go Live & Train

Launch the system across your institution. We provide comprehensive training sessions, documentation, and 24/7 support to ensure smooth adoption.
